# macos: tap-qualified retention identity and evidence-tightening round

## Summary

Five review rounds after the first convergence (roborev jobs 1749–1766,
commits `efd4bdc` through `30bce26`) produced a second wave of fixes,
recorded here because no entry covered them.

Inventory identity. The formula inventory now comes from
`brew info --installed --json=v2`, which carries the tap-qualified
`full_name`. Findings, coverage retention, the post-scan reconciliation
and the finding key all compare that identity, so a formula replaced by
another tap's build of the same short name and version is caught: the
swap invalidates the scan or retires the removed formula's advisory,
instead of the replacement inheriting it. This also fixed the cask
double-listing that made every online check on a Mac with casks discard
its findings.

Evidence rules. A matched scan row without advisory evidence marks its
kegs unknown rather than covered. Rows for skipped, refused or stale
formulas are dropped before the checked-count comparison. Accounting
failure forces completeness false. Repository URLs lose credentials at
parse time. Context rows for a finding cleared during the check's own
lifetime are trimmed at save; retained history stays. pwn.watch leads
match on every advisory id, zero_wire on exact CVE tags only — the trim
follows each source's own matching rule.

Upgrade behavior. `cleanup=False` unsets `HOMEBREW_NO_INSTALL_CLEANUP`
with `env -u` (an exported variable would otherwise be inherited).
A formula that appears only in the greedy outdated response is listed as
an ordinary update; only casks are honestly greedy-only, and the reason
text says a between-calls update is possible.
